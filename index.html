How this is built

  • ReliableAccount balances and liabilities from QuickBooks. Private client supplier payments from Ruveen’s own table, which she works from daily.
  • CalculatedPublic tour costs are not in Airtable — they sit in a separate Excel costing file per tour. These figures come from what a seat actually cost on the three spring departures that filled: $3,612 each.
  • ModelledAll of 2027, at $3,750 a seat across twenty departures, $8,642 a private client trip, $34,015 a month of client management and commissions, $53,000 of overheads, and $4.0M of revenue on 2026’s monthly shape. That is 69% cost of sales against 75% in both 2024 and 2025, so it is mildly optimistic.
  • The floorThe dotted line assumes no new sales at all from today. The 82 public tour seats and six private client trips already sold for 2027 still run, bringing in their balances and incurring their costs, which is why it falls more slowly than the running costs alone.
  • To fix itThe November session with Rubina and Ruveen puts the tour costings into Airtable. The estimated trip value column agreed on 6 October gives private clients a revenue figure to set cost against.
